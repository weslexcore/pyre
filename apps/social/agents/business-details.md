# Reference: about the business

Read this before drafting copy, captions, or visual concepts. The facts and voice here keep social posts on-brand and factually correct.

## What Pyre is

A modern communal bathhouse in Richmond, Virginia offering traditional Finnish sauna and cold plunge — contrast therapy practiced together. Pyre is positioned as a social, restorative space, not a gym or spa.

Core line, used across the site: **"The bathhouse for people who want to feel human again."**

The tagline is **SELF-CARE TOGETHER** — that pairing of the words "self-care" and "together" is intentional and recurring. Self-care isn't an isolated optimization here; it's a shared practice.

## Location

- **Address:** 1000 Westover Hills Blvd, Richmond, VA 23225
- **Site name:** Pyre @ Living Water (the neighborhood / property the bathhouse sits on)
- **Setting:** Outdoor, nature-focused. The space is built around being outside.

When showing the address in a post, lead with the street address. "Westover Hills Blvd." reads well on its own when space is tight.

## Founders

## What people do here

Three offering types — match these names exactly when referencing them:

| Offering | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Open Hours** | Self-directed. Move between saunas and cold plunges at your own pace. The "I just want to drop in" mode. |
| **Guided Sessions** | Led by certified sauna masters. Blends sauna, cold plunge, breathwork, movement, and reflective questions. A guided reset in community. |
| **Special Events** | Sauna and cold plunge layered with complementary practices: yoga, sound baths, breathwork, drumming, guided meditation. |

Also available:

- **Private Group Experiences** — up to 30 guests, on-site. Birthdays, bachelor/bachelorette, corporate, celebrations. Contact `groups@pyresauna.com`.
- **Private Rentals** — mobile sauna + cold plunge units delivered to the customer for 24 hours. One unit (6–12 people) $650, two units (12+) $1,200. Contact `rentals@pyresauna.com`.

## Facility specs

- Sauna: **traditional Finnish**, 170–195°F.
- Cold plunge: 39–50°F.
- **Sessions are 1-2 hours** of access. Within that window the common cadence is 10–20 min sauna → 1–3 min cold plunge → repeat 2–4 rounds.

## Visual / brand cues (for art direction)

Already encoded in `shared/brand.css` and `shared/tailwind.css`, but in case a post calls for specific framing:

- **Color story:** earth and ember. Pyre red (`--pyre-red #d15232`), Pyre blue (`--pyre-blue #274868`), Pyre creme (`--pyre-creme #f5f1e9`) carry most of the weight. Gold (`--pyre-gold`), sage (`--pyre-sage`), and sky (`--pyre-sky`) accent.
- **Type system:**
  - `--font-display` (PPFraktionMono Bold) for headlines — chunky, slightly geometric, holds attention.
  - `--font-mono` (PPNeueMontreal Mono) for eyebrows, captions, metadata.
  - `--font-sans` (PPNeueMontreal Regular/SemiBold) for body / lede.
  - `--font-logo` (Eckmannpsych) for the Pyre wordmark only.
- **Setting cue.** Pyre is outdoors and nature-adjacent. Imagery and visual treatments lean toward outdoor light, water, wood, steam — not white tile or studio lighting.
